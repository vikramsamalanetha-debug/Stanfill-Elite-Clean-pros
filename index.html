What's in a basic cleaning, room by room

We treat every home and business like it's our own. Here's exactly what's covered on a standard visit.

Kitchen
  • Wipe countertops
  • Wash dishes / load the dishwasher
  • Clean sink & faucets
  • Wipe cabinet fronts
  • Clean stove & burners
  • Wipe appliances
  • Empty trash & replace bag
  • Vacuum & mop floors
Living room
  • Dust all surfaces
  • Wipe coffee & side tables
  • Clean mirrors & glass
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Mop hardwood floors
  • Straighten & tidy up
Bedrooms
  • Dust all surfaces
  • Change bed linens — clean linens left out and available
  • Vacuum carpets & rugs
  • Wipe mirrors & glass
  • Vacuum & mop floors
  • Organize & tidy up
Bathrooms
  • Clean & disinfect toilet
  • Clean sink & countertops
  • Wipe mirrors & glass
  • Clean shower / tub
  • Vacuum & mop floors
  • Empty trash
